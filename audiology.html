Audiology department

Before children can learn, they need language.

Pupils are fitted with hearing aids and encouraged to listen & speak. All but a tiny minority benefit — even to hear their own voice. Most develop intelligible speech if they start early.

Dedicated rooms — hearing tests, fittings, repairs + earmould lab. Overseen by Monica Tomlin (since 2004), run by 2 locally trained teachers.
Donated tech — digital programmable aids & batteries (Deaf Kidz). Repair & calibration on site.
🚐 Mobile clinic 2025: MOU with deaf schools in Freetown & Bo; 2,000+ babies born yearly with loss. Help fund the van.
