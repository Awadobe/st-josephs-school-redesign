The school • Education for life

From first words to university.

Sierra Leone National Curriculum, adapted for deaf learners: max 8 per class, face-to-face teaching, hearing aids + speech + sign & finger-spelling. Older students mentor younger ones in chores, braiding hair, Sunday Mass.

FIRST SCHOOL • 4–8

Reception: FS1–FS2

3–4 years with trained teachers of the deaf to learn to communicate. Hearing aids + intelligible speech where possible, sign where needed.

MIDDLE • 8–15

MS1–MS3 + NPSE

National primary curriculum in tiny groups. 4–5 years, then Year 6 NPSE at ~13 (range 11–17). Academic → Junior Secondary; others → vocational.

JUNIOR SECONDARY • 13–18

JS1–JS3 + BECE

Bridge year for language gap, then national JS curriculum over 4 years. Extra tuition Sat + 3–4pm daily. BECE in agriculture, home economics, IT.

SENIOR + UNIVERSITY

WASSCE → University

Good BECE → St Joseph's Girls (adjacent) for WASSCE science / home economics / commerce, with continued deaf-teacher support. 2017: first UNIMAK enrolments. 2023: first graduates (IT, health science, accounting).
