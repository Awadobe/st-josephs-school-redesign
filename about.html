About us • Est. 1979

Forty-five years of care and special education.

St Joseph's School for the Hearing Impaired has served deaf children from all over Sierra Leone for 40+ years. 250 pupils aged 4–18, 110 boarders from remote areas, 58 staff led by Director Sister Amala Santiago.

Fully equipped Audiology Laboratory — the only such facility in Sierra Leone. In an impoverished society where disabled children are marginalised and sometimes abandoned, we offer shelter, language, schooling and vocational skills: carpentry, IT, dressmaking, hairdressing, hospitality, agriculture and animal husbandry.

History

1979Founded by Sr. Mary Sweeney with 2 teachers, 8 pupils.
1982Government recognised. Teachers trained in Netherlands '83–'86.
1986Boarding opens for 35 pupils.
1998Rebels occupy school. War ends 2001; Sisters rebuild 2002. Farm bought 1995.
2004Audiologist Monica Tomlin joins. UNIMAK partnership 2007.
2012+Mainstream secondary 2012, university 2017, Freetown outreach 2018.
2019+Sr. Amala director, new boarding 2021, solar 2022, first graduates 2023.
2025Mobile audiology clinic for Freetown, Bo & beyond.
